### Post 1 — the finding

```
I checked [N] cosmetic dental clinics in Dubai to see whether AI engines recommend them.

Not Google. ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI mode — where a growing share of patients now start.

I asked the questions a patient actually asks. "Best cosmetic dentist in Dubai." "Where should I get veneers done." "Best dental implant clinic in Dubai."

The rest weren't ranked low. They weren't there.

Here's the part that surprised me: it mostly wasn't about reputation or reviews. Several of the invisible clinics were excellent practices with strong Google rankings.

Their problem was technical. AI crawlers were blocked or couldn't read their content. Their pages weren't structured so an answer could be extracted. Their business details were inconsistent across the web, so engines couldn't resolve them as one entity.

All fixable. But you can't fix what nobody told you was broken — and there's no dashboard for this. Google gives you Search Console. AI answers give you nothing.

If you run a clinic and you're curious where you stand, comment or message me. I'll run five prompts and send you the screenshots. No charge, no pitch.
```

### Post 2 — the distinction (establishes expertise)

```
Two different things get called "AI SEO" and they need different work.

AEO — Answer Engine Optimization. Getting your content extracted as the direct answer. Featured snippets, People Also Ask, AI Overviews. Mostly on-site work: answer-first content, question-shaped headings, real tables, valid schema. Moves relatively fast.

GEO — Generative Engine Optimization. Getting cited as a source when an LLM writes its own answer. ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity. Mostly off-site: third-party references, review presence, entity consistency, original data worth citing. Slower, harder, more defensible once you have it.

Simplest way to hold the difference:

AEO wants you to BE the answer.
GEO wants you to be in the set of sources the answer was BUILT FROM.

Why it matters practically: they move on different timelines. If someone sells you a three-month programme and reports one number, ask which of these they actually did.

One more thing worth knowing. Run the same prompt through ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and AI Overviews, and the overlap in which domains get cited is roughly 11%.

Four platforms. Four almost entirely separate source lists.

So "we'll make you visible in AI" isn't a strategy. Visibility is won engine by engine, and it has to be reported that way.
```

### Post 3 — the measurement gap (the differentiator)

```
Here's the uncomfortable question to ask anyone selling you AI search visibility:

"How will you measure it?"

Most of the time the conversation gets vague fast. And that vagueness isn't dishonesty — it's a real structural problem. There is no Search Console for AI answers. No official dashboard. No impressions report.

Worse, these systems are non-deterministic. Ask ChatGPT the same question twice and you can get two different answers, with different sources cited. So a single check tells you almost nothing.

The only thing that works is boring and manual:

→ A fixed set of prompts — 40 to 50, the sentences patients actually use
→ Run three times per prompt, per engine, because of that non-determinism
→ Controlled conditions: logged out, consistent location, same time of week
→ Two numbers recorded: how often you're named, and how often you're cited with a link
→ Reported per engine, never averaged
→ Same set, same conditions, every month

That's it. No clever tool required to start. A browser and a spreadsheet.

It's tedious. It's also the only way to tell the difference between real movement and a lucky screenshot.

If a vendor can't describe their measurement method in specific terms, they're guessing — and you're paying for the guess.
```

### Posting rules

- Short lines, generous whitespace. Dense paragraphs die in the feed.
- First line is the whole game — it's all that shows before "see more".
- No hashtag spam. Two or three at most, or none.
- No external link in the post body; LinkedIn suppresses reach. Put it in the
  first comment if needed.
- Never fabricate a number. If you checked 12 clinics, say 12.
- Post 1 → then wait 3–4 days → Post 2 → then 3–4 days → Post 3.
